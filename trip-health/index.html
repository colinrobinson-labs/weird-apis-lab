How levels work

Every level comes from a published threshold: the US Heat Index bands, WHO UV categories, the US AQI, CDC altitude guidance, and UK government travel advice. The overall level is the highest single risk, never an average, so a serious risk can't be diluted. We never say "safe". The best level is "Normal".

Reliability grades

A = official measurement or record. B = a reputable forecast model. C = country-level guidance. D = thin or out of date. Every card links its source.
